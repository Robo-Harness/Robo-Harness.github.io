Abstract摘要

Reasoning that lands in the physical world让推理稳定落到物理世界

Language models can write programs that drive robots, yet the same model can score several times higher or lower depending on the system built around it. KnowinAgent-RoboDojo treats that system as the object of design. The agent acts by submitting short programs; a trackable execution layer turns controller limits into planning constraints; budget governance is centred on native action steps and rejects premature completion; and an evidence-grounded evolution loop converts a few practice episodes into frozen, verifiable task knowledge.

Across all 54 task variants of the RoboDojo simulation benchmark, KnowinAgent-RoboDojo reaches 87.60, the highest score under the official five-dimension formula. Most tasks are mastered with just two training episodes, and 11 of 12 randomized-layout variants are solved with no additional training.

语言模型已经能够编写程序驱动机器人，但同一个模型在不同系统之上的表现可以相差数倍。KnowinAgent-RoboDojo 把这套系统本身作为设计对象：智能体以短程序作为动作；可跟踪执行层把控制器的跟踪极限转化为规划约束；预算治理以原生动作步数为中心，并拦截过早的“完成”判断；证据化的进化闭环把少量练习回合沉淀为冻结、可校验的任务知识。

在 RoboDojo 仿真基准全部 54 个任务变体上，KnowinAgent-RoboDojo 取得 87.60 分，为官方五维计分公式下的最高成绩。多数任务只需 2 个训练回合即可掌握，12 个随机布局变体中有 11 个无需任何额外训练。

Training训练

Most tasks are mastered in two training episodes多数任务两个训练回合即可掌握

Each training episode is a practice run with score feedback; a review agent that never touches the simulator then distils the evidence into skills and lessons, which are frozen before testing. We count the training episodes behind every frozen knowledge version, following its full lineage.每个训练回合是一局带分数反馈的练习；之后由不接触仿真器的复盘智能体把证据提炼为技能与经验，冻结后再进入测试。我们沿完整的知识链路，统计每份冻结知识背后的训练回合数。

Method方法

The system beneath the model模型之下的系统

Five core techniques form a complete loop from perception and decision-making to execution and evolution.五项核心技术，构成从感知、决策、执行到进化的完整闭环。

1Embodied Experience Self-Evolution具身经验自进化

Distils transferable skills from interaction trajectories and constrains every knowledge update with execution evidence, achieving few-shot mastery and cross-layout generalization.从交互轨迹中归纳可迁移技能，以执行证据约束每一次知识更新，实现少样本掌握与跨布局泛化。

Median 2 training episodes · 11/12 random layouts reused训练回合中位数 2 · 11/12 随机布局直接复用

2Active Perception with Metacognitive Decision-Making主动感知与元认知决策

Task demands drive viewpoint selection, with hierarchical judgement between action-level correction and plan-level replanning.由任务需求驱动视角选择，在动作层修正与方案层重规划之间进行分层判断。

Millimetre offsets corrected on the spot毫米级偏差就地修正

3Metric-Grade Spatial Perception度量级空间感知

Learned priors and multi-view geometric constraints are solved jointly and verified in closed loop by reprojection consistency, reaching sub-centimetre to millimetre localization.学习先验与多视几何约束联合求解，以重投影一致性闭环校验，实现亚厘米至毫米级定位。

Median error 8.3 mm head camera · 0.4 mm wrist定位误差中位数：头部 8.3 mm · 腕部 0.4 mm

4Budget-Constrained Long-Horizon Planning预算约束下的长程规划

Makes the physical interaction budget an explicit part of planning and, with bimanual parallel scheduling, carries long-horizon tasks through to completion.将物理交互预算显式纳入规划，结合双臂并行调度，保障长程任务的完整闭环。

Every step leaves room to finish the task每一步都留有完成任务的空间

5Counterfactual Review and Pre-Commit Verification反事实复盘与前置校验

Attributes success to its causes and moves post-hoc corrections forward into pre-execution predictions, so irreversible actions succeed on the first attempt.对成功进行归因分析，将事后修正前移为事前预测，使不可逆操作在首次执行即可成立。

Pouring: 5/5 test episodes succeeded on both layouts倒水任务标准与随机布局均 5/5 局成功
